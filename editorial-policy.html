How we maintain content

Editorial Policy

Our goal is for each tool to be understandable, testable, and clear about what it does not calculate.

Formulas and rates

We explain formulas and important assumptions near the calculator. For regulated or changing rates, we prioritize primary government sources and link to them. Rate-dependent tools should identify their applicable date or tax year.

Testing and limitations

Calculator changes should be checked against known sample calculations, boundary cases, and invalid input. Estimates should not be described as guaranteed, personalized advice, or an official government result.

Corrections and updates

When an error or changed source is identified, the affected calculation and explanation should be reviewed and corrected. The site owner should publish a monitored contact address on the Contact page before launch so visitors can report issues.

Independence and transparency

MapleToolbox does not publish fabricated reviews, ratings, endorsements, statistics, or search-volume claims. The current project does not identify an editor, company, or professional credential; no such affiliations or credentials are implied by this policy.
