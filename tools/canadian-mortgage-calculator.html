How this estimate works

The payment is calculated using the mortgage principal, interest rate converted from a nominal Canadian rate compounded twice yearly, and the selected amortization. Accelerated biweekly payments are set to half the regular monthly payment.

Not a mortgage offer or lending qualification. Insurance, property tax, fees, and lender-specific terms are not included.
