How it works

The estimate uses A = P(1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ, where P is the initial balance, r is the annual rate, n is the compounding frequency, and t is the number of years.

Assumes a constant rate and excludes fees, taxes, inflation, and additional deposits. Actual returns are not guaranteed.
