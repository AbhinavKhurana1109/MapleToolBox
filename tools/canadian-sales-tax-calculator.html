How it works

This estimate applies the selected jurisdiction's general combined rate to your taxable price. Actual tax can differ depending on the product, service, and place-of-supply rules.

Rates are the CRA's general rates applicable in 2026 for supplies made on or after April 1, 2025. For the official rate table and place-of-supply guidance, see the CRA's GST/HST calculator and provincial rate table and place-of-supply information.
