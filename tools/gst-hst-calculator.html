How it works

The estimate applies the selected province or territory's general combined GST/HST and provincial sales tax rate. If tax is included, it backs the estimated tax out of the entered total.

Rates shown are the CRA's general rates for supplies made on or after April 1, 2025, applicable in 2026. Tax depends on supply type and place of supply. See the CRA's GST/HST calculator and provincial rate table and place-of-supply guidance.
