2026 tax-year assumptions

This calculator applies the federal and selected provincial 2026 progressive tax brackets to the entered gross income, assuming gross salary equals taxable income. It estimates tax before personal credits and does not include CPP/QPP, EI, surtaxes, benefits, deductions, or individual circumstances. The displayed amount after estimated income tax is therefore not take-home pay, guaranteed tax owed, or tax advice.

Quebec is not available in this estimate because its 2026 provincial bracket rates could not be verified against an accessible official source during this audit. Consult Revenu Québec's income tax rates or use the official province-specific tools.
