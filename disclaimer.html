Tax and sales tax

Tax rates and calculations can depend on tax year, province, residence, type of supply, credits, deductions, and other rules. Canadian salary results are simplified bracket estimates before personal credits and payroll contributions, not a payroll calculation or a prediction of tax payable. Sales tax results use general rates and do not cover every taxable, exempt, zero-rated, or place-of-supply case. Consult the linked official source or a tax professional.
