About the project

About MapleToolbox

Useful calculations should be easy to understand, easy to access, and honest about their limits.

What you’ll find here

MapleToolbox is a collection of free online calculators for Canadian tax and money questions, everyday planning, and common unit conversions. Each calculator explains what it calculates and calls out assumptions that can affect the result.

The tools run in your browser and do not require an account. Financial and tax results are estimates, not professional advice or guaranteed outcomes.

How we approach accuracy

Where a calculator depends on official rates, we link to the relevant public source and identify the applicable year or rate date. Mathematical tools show their formulas or conversion factors. If a rate cannot be verified from an official source, we avoid presenting it as authoritative.

See our editorial policy, disclaimer, and privacy policy.
