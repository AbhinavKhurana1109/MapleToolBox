Frequently asked questions

A little more about how MapleToolbox works.

Are these calculators free to use?

Yes. The calculators are free, run in your browser, and do not require an account.

How accurate are the results?

Results are calculated from the values and assumptions shown on each tool. Planning and tax results are estimates; actual outcomes depend on your circumstances and applicable rules.

Which tax year do the Canadian tax tools use?

The GST/HST and sales tax tools show the rates used. The salary calculator is labelled for the 2026 tax year and links to the Canada Revenue Agency’s published brackets. Always check the linked official source for updates.

Do you save the numbers I enter?

Calculations happen locally in your browser. The site does not send your entered values to a server.
